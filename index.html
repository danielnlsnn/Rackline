Common questions

Can I visit my servers in person?

Yes. After registration you get a personal badge and can access your rack at any time. Visitors need to be announced 24 hours in advance.

What happens if a power feed fails?

Every rack has two independent feeds from separate UPS systems, backed by diesel generators. Servers with dual power supplies keep running without interruption.

Do you support my own IP addresses?

Yes. We can announce your own IP ranges via BGP, or assign addresses from our pool if you don't have any.

How is remote hands work billed?

The first 30 minutes each month are included. After that, we bill in 15-minute steps at €22 per step. You approve every job before we start.
